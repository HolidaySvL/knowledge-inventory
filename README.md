# AI Knowledge Inventory

## Preparing Enterprise Knowledge Before AI Answers

Most enterprise AI projects start with a chatbot.

This project starts one step earlier.

Before a company can build a reliable AI assistant, it needs to understand whether its internal documents are complete, current, traceable, and safe to use.

**AI Knowledge Inventory** is a deployed web app that helps turn messy business documents into a structured, reviewable knowledge inventory before they enter a knowledge base, RAG system, or enterprise AI assistant.

---

## Why I Built This

Enterprise AI does not fail only because the model is weak.

It often fails because the source knowledge is messy.

Internal documents may be outdated, duplicated, incomplete, ownerless, or inconsistent. If these documents are directly ingested into a chatbot, the AI may produce confident but unreliable answers.

So I designed a workflow that asks a different question:

> Before asking AI to answer, can we first check whether the knowledge itself is ready?

---

## What the App Does

The app allows users to upload business documents or a ZIP folder and automatically generates:

* A knowledge inventory
* A document risk assessment
* A knowledge-base readiness dashboard
* A process map
* A downloadable report bundle

It helps identify:

* Which documents are usable
* Which documents need human review
* Which business processes are covered
* Which knowledge assets are ready for future AI ingestion
* Where validation is required before publishing knowledge

---

## Core Workflow

```text
Review existing documents
        ↓
Validate document quality
        ↓
Process qualified content
        ↓
Prepare for knowledge ingestion
        ↓
Support future RAG / enterprise AI assistant
```

The key principle is simple:

> AI can help read, organize, and draft.
> Critical judgment should still remain with humans.
